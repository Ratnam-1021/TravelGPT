# New York City Destination & Travel Guide

## Overview
New York City comprises 5 boroughs sitting where the Hudson River meets the Atlantic Ocean. At its core is Manhattan, a densely populated borough that's among the world's major commercial, financial, and cultural centers.

## Top Attractions & Highlights
- **Central Park & Bethesda Terrace**: 843 acres of green meadows, lakes, and walking trails.
- **Statue of Liberty & Ellis Island**: Scenic ferry trip to iconic historic monuments.
- **Times Square & Broadway**: Neon lights, theater shows, and midtown energy.
- **Empire State Building & Summit One Vanderbilt**: Breathtaking skyscraper observation decks.
- **Metropolitan Museum of Art (The Met) & MoMA**: World-renowned art collections.
- **Brooklyn Bridge & DUMBO**: Walk across the historic suspension bridge for skyline photos.

## Neighborhoods & Accommodation
- **Luxury**: Midtown / Upper East Side (The Plaza, St. Regis) ($650-$1400/night).
- **Mid-Range**: Soho, Chelsea, Financial District ($220-$400/night).
- **Budget**: Queens (Long Island City), Brooklyn, or budget pods ($100-$180/night).

## Food & Dining Costs
- Budget meal (Dollar slice, Bagel with lox, Halal Cart): $5 - $15
- Mid-range restaurant dinner: $45 - $90 per person
- Broadway pre-theater fine dining: $120 - $250 per person

## Transportation
- **MTA Subway MetroCard / OMNY Contactless**: $2.90 per ride, $34 for 7-day unlimited pass.
- **Yellow Cabs / Lyft**: Readily available, base fares start around $3.50 + mileage.

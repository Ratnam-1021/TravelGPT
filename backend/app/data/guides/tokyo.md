# Tokyo Destination & Travel Guide

## Overview
Tokyo, Japan’s bustling capital, mixes ultra-modern skyscrapers and neon-lit streets with historic temples, serene shrines, and world-class culinary experiences.

## Top Attractions & Highlights
- **Senso-ji Temple & Asakusa**: Tokyo’s oldest Buddhist temple surrounded by vibrant market stalls.
- **Shibuya Crossing & Harajuku**: Famous scramble intersection, Takeshita Street, and youth fashion hub.
- **TeamLab Planets / Borderless**: Immersive digital art installations in Toyosu/Odaiba.
- **Shinjuku & Omoide Yokocho**: High-energy night district with izakayas, bar alleys, and skyline observatories.
- **Tsukiji Outer Market & Toyosu**: Fresh sushi, street food snacks, and tuna auction viewings.
- **Meiji Shrine & Yoyogi Park**: Tranquil forest park right in the middle of urban Shibuya.

## Neighborhoods & Accommodation
- **Luxury**: Ginza / Tokyo Station (Aman Tokyo, Park Hyatt Shinjuku) ($600-$1500/night).
- **Mid-Range**: Shinjuku, Shibuya, Akasaka business & boutique hotels ($120-$280/night).
- **Budget**: Asakusa, Ueno, Capsule hotels & guesthouses ($40-$90/night).

## Food & Dining Costs
- Budget meal (Ramen, Gyudon bowl, Konbini meal): ¥600 - ¥1,500 ($4 - $10)
- Mid-range Izakaya or Sushi set: ¥3,000 - ¥7,000 ($20 - $48) per person
- Omakase Sushi / Kaiseki: ¥15,000 - ¥35,000+ ($100 - $240+) per person

## Transportation
- **Suica / Pasmo IC Card**: Rechargeable card for Tokyo Metro, Toei Subway, JR Yamanote Line ($8-$15/day).
- **JR Pass**: Recommended if traveling intercity to Kyoto/Osaka on Shinkansen.

## Best Time to Visit
Cherry Blossom Season (Late March - Early April) and Autumn Foliage (November - December).

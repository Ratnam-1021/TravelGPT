# Bali Destination & Travel Guide

## Overview
Bali, Indonesia is famed for its forested volcanic mountains, iconic rice paddies, beaches, coral reefs, and rich Hindu spiritual culture.

## Top Attractions & Highlights
- **Ubud Rice Terraces & Monkey Forest**: Cultural heart of Bali with lush green landscapes and artisan shops.
- **Uluwatu Temple & Kecak Fire Dance**: Dramatic cliffside temple overlooking the Indian Ocean.
- **Tanah Lot Temple**: Historic offshore pilgrimage temple famous for sunset photography.
- **Nusa Penida Day Trip**: Kelingking T-Rex Beach, Broken Beach, and crystal clear snorkeling waters.
- **Seminyak & Canggu Beach Clubs**: Potato Head, Finns Beach Club, surfing, and sunset lounge vibes.

## Neighborhoods & Accommodation
- **Luxury Villa**: Seminyak, Nusa Dua, Uluwatu private pool villas ($250-$800/night).
- **Mid-Range**: Ubud, Canggu boutique guesthouses and eco-resorts ($60-$160/night).
- **Budget**: Kuta, Sanur hostels and homestays ($15-$45/night).

## Food & Dining Costs
- Budget local warung (Nasi Goreng / Babi Guling): $2 - $6
- Mid-range cafe or beach restaurant: $12 - $30 per person
- Upscale dining & beach club minimum spend: $50 - $120 per person

## Transportation
- **Scooter / Motorbike Rental**: $5 - $9 per day (international license required).
- **Private Car Driver / Grab / Gojek**: $35 - $55 per full day rental with driver.

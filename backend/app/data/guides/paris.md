# Paris Destination & Travel Guide

## Overview
Paris, France's capital, is a global center for art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. Its 19th-century cityscape is crisscrossed by wide boulevards and the River Seine.

## Top Attractions & Highlights
- **Eiffel Tower**: Iconic symbol of Paris. Best visited early morning or evening for light shows.
- **Louvre Museum**: World's largest art museum, home to the Mona Lisa and Venus de Milo.
- **Le Marais**: Historic district filled with boutique shops, galleries, and falafel spots.
- **Montmartre & Sacré-Cœur**: Artistic hilltop neighborhood with sweeping city views.
- **Musée d'Orsay**: Renowned impressionist masterworks inside a magnificent converted railway station.
- **Notre-Dame Cathedral & Seine Cruise**: Picturesque riverside walks and sunset boat tours.

## Neighborhoods & Accommodation
- **Luxury**: 8th Arrondissement (Champs-Élysées), Le Meurice, Ritz Paris ($500-$1200/night).
- **Mid-Range**: 5th/6th Arrondissement (Latin Quarter/Saint-Germain), boutique hotels ($180-$350/night).
- **Budget**: 10th/11th Arrondissement (Canal Saint-Martin/Bastille) or hostels ($60-$120/night).

## Food & Dining Costs
- Budget meal (boulangerie sandwich / crêpe): €6 - €12
- Mid-range bistro dinner (3 courses with wine): €35 - €65 per person
- Fine dining / Michelin star: €120 - €300+ per person
- Espresso at cafe bar: €2 - €3.50

## Transportation
- **Navigo Pass / Metro Ticket T+**: €2.15 per metro ride or ~€30 for weekly unlimited pass.
- **Taxis & Rideshare**: Uber and G7 Taxis available; flat-rate from CDG Airport to city center (€56 - €65).

## Best Time to Visit
Spring (April–June) and Autumn (September–October) offer mild weather, blooming gardens, and fewer crowds than summer.

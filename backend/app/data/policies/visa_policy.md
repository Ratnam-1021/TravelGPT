# Travel Policy: Visa Requirements & Entry Formalities

## Schengen Area (France, Italy, Spain, Germany, etc.)
- **ETIAS Requirement**: Starting 2025/2026, travelers from visa-exempt countries (USA, UK, Canada, Australia) must apply online for ETIAS authorization (€7 fee, valid for 3 years).
- **Schengen Short-Stay Visa**: Non-exempt passport holders require a Schengen Visa prior to departure (valid up to 90 days within any 180-day period). Passport must have at least 3 months validity beyond intended departure.

## Japan (Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka)
- **Visa Exemption**: Citizens of 68 countries (including USA, EU, UK, Canada, Australia) enjoy visa-free short stays for up to 90 days.
- **Visit Japan Web**: Digital entry registration for immigration and customs declarations prior to arrival.

## United States (New York, Los Angeles, Miami)
- **ESTA (Visa Waiver Program)**: Travelers from 41 VWP countries must obtain approved ESTA online ($21 fee) at least 72 hours before flight.
- **B1/B2 Visitor Visa**: Travelers from non-VWP countries require an interview and approved B1/B2 tourist visa.

## Indonesia (Bali)
- **Visa on Arrival (VoA)**: Available electronically (e-VoA) or at airport arrival counter for 90+ nationalities ($35 USD / IDR 500,000, valid for 30 days, extendable once).
- **Tourist Levy**: IDR 150,000 (~$10 USD) mandatory Bali tourist entrance fee payable online via Love Bali portal.

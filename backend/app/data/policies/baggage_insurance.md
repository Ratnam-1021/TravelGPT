# Travel Policy: Baggage Allowances & Travel Insurance

## Airline Baggage Rules & Standards
- **Carry-On Limits**: Most international airlines allow 1 carry-on bag (up to 7kg–10kg / 15-22 lbs) plus 1 personal item (laptop bag/purse). Low-cost carriers (EasyJet, Ryanair, AirAsia) strictly enforce weight and size limits.
- **Checked Luggage Standard**: Economy tickets usually include 1 piece up to 23kg (50 lbs). Transpacific/long-haul flights may allow 2 pieces. Overweight fees range from $50 to $150 per bag.

## Travel Insurance Mandates & Recommendations
- **Medical Coverage**: Minimum $50,000 to $100,000 recommended emergency medical & evacuation coverage.
- **Trip Cancellation & Delay**: Covers non-refundable hotel reservations, flight changes, and lost baggage claims.
- **Schengen Visa Requirement**: Proof of travel insurance with minimum €30,000 medical coverage is mandatory for Schengen visa applicants.

## Safety & Health Checklist
- Check routine vaccinations (Hepatitis A/B, Tetanus, Typhoid for Southeast Asia).
- Always keep digital copies of passport, visa, credit cards, and emergency contact details in secure cloud storage.

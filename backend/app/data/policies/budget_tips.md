# Travel Policy: Budget Allocation & Cost Optimization Tips

## Golden Rule of Travel Budgeting (40-30-15-15 Rule)
- **40% Accommodation**: Hotels, airbnbs, or hostels.
- **30% Food & Dining**: Breakfast, lunch, dinner, drinks, and culinary tours.
- **15% Transportation**: Flights, trains, metro passes, rideshares, and car rentals.
- **15% Activities & Contingency**: Museum entry fees, guided tours, shopping, and emergency reserve funds.

## Cost Saving Strategies
1. **Fly Mid-Week**: Flights on Tuesdays and Wednesdays are often 15-25% cheaper than weekend flights.
2. **City Passes & Public Transit**: Purchase multi-day transit passes (e.g. Navigo in Paris, Suica/Pass in Tokyo, MetroCard in NYC) instead of point-to-point tickets or taxis.
3. **Local Bakeries & Street Markets**: Grab breakfast or lunch at local bakeries or food markets for high quality at lower prices.
4. **Free Museum Days**: Check local tourist boards for free admission days (e.g., first Sunday of the month in Paris).
